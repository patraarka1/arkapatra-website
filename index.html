I am a Senior Research Fellow at the Saha Institute of Nuclear Physics, working on magnetism and Heusler alloys.

My research focuses on quaternary Heusler alloys, with an emphasis on structural disorder, magnetism, and transport behavior for various applications. The work is primarily experimental, involving synthesis and detailed characterization of these alloys. The results are compared with first-principles predictions from theoretical collaborators, to understand disorder-driven deviations from the expected electronic and magnetic ground states.

Outside research, I enjoy reading story books and touring new places. I was also the President of the Research Fellow Association (RFA) at SINP.
